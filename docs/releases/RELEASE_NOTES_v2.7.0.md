# Avirzo v2.7.0 — African Story Intelligence Engine

## Purpose
Connect the African Roots Foundation to story structure, characters, research, scenes and cinematic language.

## Added
- Story Intelligence analysis endpoint: `/api/ai/story-intelligence`.
- Foundation readiness score and missing-field guidance.
- Cultural-anchor propagation into scene intelligence.
- Character arc prompts based on the project bible.
- Scene-by-scene story goals, continuity focus and research flags.
- Pre-1994 anachronism checks for common modern technology/brands.
- Evidence discipline for documented history, oral tradition, folklore and creative reconstruction.
- New Story Intelligence panel in the studio.
- `rootsFoundation` persisted in normalized cloud projects.

## Product principle
Avirzo remains an African filmmaking studio first. AI intelligence should strengthen cultural specificity and filmmaking quality, not flatten stories into generic pan-African imagery.
