# Avirzo v2.9.0 — Research → Story → Production Bridge

The Research Story Bridge makes the project's heritage evidence traceable through story development and scene planning.

It surfaces documented facts, material culture, oral/community memory and uncertainties, carries the evidence/verification status and source steward, and identifies scenes whose text appears relevant to each research block.

The bridge is deliberately assistive rather than authoritative: keyword matching does not establish historical truth. Research provenance and community review remain the authority.
