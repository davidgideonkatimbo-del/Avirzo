Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version. Avirzo is an AI film workspace. We collect what we need to run your account, save your projects and media, generate scenes, voices and music, and take payment. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads. You can download or delete your data at any time from the Profile page.

1. Who we are

Avirzo (“we”, “us”) is operated by David Gideon Katimbo, based in Uganda. Questions about this policy: burstcomepsp@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: email address, display name, password (stored by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text)To create and secure your account.
Your projects and media: scripts, scenes, characters, world notes, reference images, voice recordings, generated video, audio and exportsTo provide the service and let you return to your work.
Prompts and settings you send to AI featuresTo generate scenes, keyframes, voices and music, and to keep characters consistent.
Billing details: plan, status, the date your paid period ends, credit balance and usage, and a record of each payment (item, amount, currency, status and Pesapal reference). Card numbers and mobile-money PINs are handled by Pesapal; we never see or store themTo provide paid plans, credits and receipts.
Security records: a one-way hash of your IP address, user-agent, and blocked or rate-limited requestsTo prevent abuse and fraud. Raw IP addresses are not stored.
Agreement record: the version of the Terms you accepted and whenTo show you accepted our rules.
Reports you send about contentTo review and act on them.

3. How we use it

  • Run, secure and improve Avirzo.
  • Send your prompts, reference images and text to the AI providers below so they can create what you asked for.
  • Prevent misuse, enforce our Terms and respond to reports.
  • Take payments, issue refunds and keep required financial records.
  • Send essential account emails (confirmation, password reset, billing). We do not send marketing without your consent.

We do not use your private projects to train our own models, and we do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the purpose shown:

ProviderPurpose

Content you send to an AI provider is also handled under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from serious harm.

5. How long we keep it

  • Projects and media: until you delete them or your account.
  • Security records: only as long as needed to detect and investigate abuse.
  • Financial records: as long as the law requires us to keep them.

6. Deleting your account

Open Profile → Data & safety → Delete account. We delete your media files and remove your projects, credits and account. Copies in provider backups are overwritten on their normal cycle. If you cannot sign in, email us from your registered address.

7. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, download and delete your data from the Profile page, or by email. Depending on where you live (including Uganda under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and the EU/UK under GDPR) you may also object to or restrict certain processing. We will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers

Our providers run servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside Uganda. We choose providers with appropriate safeguards.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, private storage with per-user access rules, rate limits and abuse monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

10. Children

Avirzo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and ask you to review it in the app.
