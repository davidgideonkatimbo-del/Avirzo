# Avirzo v2.5.0 — AI Filmmaking Intelligence

## Director Copilot
- Narrative beat analysis and production readiness scoring
- Camera, shot size, lighting, sound and transition suggestions
- One-click application of an AI scene plan to the storyboard
- Character continuity audit
- Research readiness audit with historical/heritage safeguards
- Pre-1994 anachronism detection for common modern references

## Design principle
The intelligence layer is deterministic and auditable. It does not present generated guesses as verified historical facts and does not claim source verification where none occurred.
