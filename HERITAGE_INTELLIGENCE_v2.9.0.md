# Avirzo v2.9.0 — Heritage Intelligence Layer

This pass strengthens Avirzo's defining product moat: African-rooted filmmaking with explicit provenance and cultural context.

## Added
- Heritage Passport fields for evidence level, verification status, community/source steward, provenance note and creative liberties.
- Clear separation between documented history, oral/community memory and creative reconstruction.
- Community-review / verification messaging before production when research is not fully verified.
- Heritage metadata remains part of the existing project research payload and therefore travels into storyboard and generation workflows.

## Guardrails
- No new generic AI-video workflow was introduced.
- No claim of factual verification is made by the client; verification remains a creator/community responsibility.
- Existing story intelligence, research and generation APIs remain unchanged.
