# Avirzo v2.9.0 — Character + World Continuity

## Purpose

This layer connects the Character Bible and World Bible to scene-level production review. It is designed to catch continuity drift before a creator spends generation usage.

## What it checks

- recurring character continuity rules and relationships
- visual identity, clothing, language and possessions when defined
- World Bible continuity locks
- scene-to-scene primary-character changes
- camera-language changes between adjacent scenes
- possible deliberate changes such as clothing, language or appearance changes
- possible pre-1994 anachronisms
- missing roots anchors that make cultural continuity harder to evaluate

## Important boundary

The audit detects **production continuity risks**. It does not establish cultural or historical truth. Research sources and community review remain authoritative for culturally sensitive or historical claims.

## Cost protection

This is a pre-generation review layer. It does not automatically regenerate provider jobs and does not claim that a continuity warning is a provider failure.
